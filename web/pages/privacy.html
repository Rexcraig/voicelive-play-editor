1. Who we are

The VoiceLive Play Editor (“the Editor”) at occhiphoto.com/vlp is operated by Verona Strategic LLC, a Florida limited liability company (“we”, “us”). The Editor is an independent tool and is not made or endorsed by TC-Helicon or Music Tribe.

2. What data we collect

The Editor runs in your web browser. It has no user accounts, and it does not send your presets, settings or recordings to us, except a preset you choose to share in Community. What it handles:

  • Data kept on your device. Your set lists, the list of presets read from your pedal, and a log of your saves are stored in your browser’s local storage. Preset backups are written to a folder you choose, or downloaded to your computer. None of this leaves your device.
  • Your pedal. With your permission, given in your browser, the Editor talks to your pedal over MIDI. That exchange happens between your browser and the pedal only.
  • Hosting. The Editor’s files are served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical information about each request (such as IP address, browser type and time) to deliver and protect the site.
  • Community, only if you use it. When you share a preset, we store the preset itself, its name, the description and genres you add, the display name you choose, the date, and the email address you signed in with. Everyone who uses the Editor can see the preset, its description and your display name; your email address is never shown to them. We keep your email so you can delete your own presets and so we can stop abuse. If you report a preset, we store the report, your reason and your email address.
  • Sign-in, while access is restricted. Access to the Editor is currently limited to invited people through Cloudflare Access. Cloudflare Access receives the email address you sign in with and sets a cookie that keeps you signed in.

3. What we do not collect

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. The Editor’s fonts are hosted on our own site, not loaded from a font service.
  • No audio. Nothing you sing or play is recorded or uploaded.
  • No payment information. The Editor is free.

4. How we use data

We use the hosting and sign-in information described above only to deliver the Editor, to keep it secure, and to control who can reach it while access is restricted. We use Community information only to run Community: to show shared presets, to let people delete their own, and to deal with reports and abuse. We do not use any of it for marketing or profiling.

5. Legal basis for processing (GDPR)

Where the EU or UK GDPR applies, we rely on our legitimate interest in delivering and securing the Editor for hosting and sign-in data, and on your request to publish a preset (performance of our agreement with you) for Community data. The Editor’s access to your MIDI devices happens only with the permission you give in your browser, which you can withdraw in your browser’s site settings at any time.

6. Data retention

  • Data on your device stays until you delete it. Clearing this site’s data in your browser removes your set lists, pedal list and save log. Backup files stay in the folder you chose until you delete them.
  • A preset you share stays in Community until you delete it or a moderator deletes it. A preset a moderator hides is kept, with the information above, until it is deleted. Deleting a preset also deletes any reports about it.
  • To enforce the daily sharing limit we log the time of each share with your email address, and delete those entries after two days. If a moderator stops you from sharing, we keep your email address on that list until the decision is reversed.
  • Hosting and sign-in records are kept by Cloudflare under its own retention schedule.

7. Data sharing and third parties

We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not share data with advertisers or data brokers. Our only service provider for the Editor is Cloudflare, Inc. (hosting, security, Cloudflare Access, and the database that stores Community presets, located in the United States), which processes data under its own privacy policy.

8. Security

The Editor is served only over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Because your presets and set lists stay on your own device, their security also depends on the security of that device and browser.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you. Data on your device is under your control and can be deleted by clearing this site’s data in your browser, and you can delete presets you shared from the Community tab. For anything else, email us at {{CONTACT}} and we will respond within 30 days.

11. Children

The Editor is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
